Support this project
====================

progressbar2 is maintained by `Rick van Hattem <https://github.com/wolph>`_ in
his own time. Most of that time goes on terminals nobody tests against and on
keeping the bar out of your logging.

Maintenance is the unglamorous half of an open source package. It is invisible
when it goes well, and the only feedback is silence.

If the package saved you an afternoon, a tip covers an hour of issue triage.

- `Ko-fi <https://ko-fi.com/wolph_gh>`_ for a one-off tip
- `GitHub Sponsors <https://github.com/sponsors/wolph>`_ for a recurring one
- `thanks.dev <https://thanks.dev/u/gh/wolph>`_ if your employer funds its
  dependency tree

Nothing here buys a response time or a support contract. It buys hours on the
parts of the package nobody else wants to look at.
